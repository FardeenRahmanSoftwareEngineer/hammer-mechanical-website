Smart Home & Controls in Hamilton

Smart thermostats, zoning, and automation upgrades installed by the same licensed team that handles your wiring and HVAC, so every device is set up right the first time.

Smart Thermostat Setup

We install and configure smart thermostats from the major brands, verifying compatibility with your furnace, A/C, or heat pump before anything goes on the wall. Because our technicians know both the low-voltage wiring and the equipment behind it, you avoid the short-cycling and missing C-wire headaches that come with DIY installs. Before we leave, we walk you through schedules, eco settings, and remote access from your phone.

Zoning System Installation

Zoning gives each area of your home its own temperature control, a practical fix for Hamilton's older two-storey homes, finished basements, and additions that never match the rest of the house. We install the dampers, zone panels, and thermostats, then balance the airflow so every zone actually delivers what its thermostat asks for. The result is even comfort room to room without running your system harder than it needs to.

Home Automation & Control Upgrades

Because we're electricians as well as HVAC techs, we can take automation beyond the thermostat: smart switches and dimmers, connected leak sensors, and controls that tie your comfort systems together. We replace outdated or unreliable control components, tidy up the wiring behind them, and configure everything to work from one app instead of five. Tell us what you want your home to do, and we'll recommend practical upgrades that fit your budget, not a gadget catalogue.
