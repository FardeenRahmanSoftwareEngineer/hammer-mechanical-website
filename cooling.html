Cooling & Air Conditioning in Hamilton

Certified HVAC technicians keeping Hamilton homes cool through humid Southern Ontario summers, with right-sized installs, fast repairs and maintenance that keeps your AC ready for the heat.

Central AC Installation

We size every central air conditioner to your home's square footage, insulation and layout, then install it for quiet operation and consistent cooling on the hottest Hamilton afternoons. A properly matched system pulls humidity out of the air, cools faster, and runs years longer than an oversized guess. You get clear options and honest pricing before any equipment is ordered.

Air Conditioner Repair & Troubleshooting

Warm air from the vents, a unit that won't start, or new rattles and buzzing all have a real cause — and our technicians find it instead of swapping parts on a hunch. We explain what failed, what the fix costs, and whether repair or replacement makes better financial sense for your system's age.

Heat Pump Installation & Service

A modern heat pump cools in summer and heats in winter from one efficient system, which makes it a smart fit for many Southern Ontario homes. We install and service heat pumps with careful attention to sizing, airflow and controls, and when performance drifts we check refrigerant charge, pressures and airflow to bring comfort back.

Ductless Mini-Split Systems

Finished basements, additions, and older Hamilton homes without ductwork are exactly where ductless mini-splits shine. We plan head placement for balanced, quiet cooling and install every line set cleanly, and if an existing unit underperforms we diagnose the controls, drainage, coils and refrigerant to restore full output.

Refrigerant Leak Detection & Recharge

Low refrigerant means weak cooling, rising hydro bills, and a compressor working far harder than it should. We trace the lines to locate the actual leak, repair it so the system holds its charge, then recharge to manufacturer specifications and verify cooling performance before we leave.

AC Coil Cleaning & Replacement

Your coils are the heat exchangers that make cooling possible, and a season of dust, cottonwood fluff and grass clippings can choke them badly enough to cause icing and higher bills. We clean condenser and evaporator coils safely to restore heat transfer, and if a coil is corroded or leaking we lay out repair-versus-replacement options honestly.

Seasonal AC Maintenance Plans

A spring tune-up is the cheapest cooling insurance you can buy: we test electrical components, clean key parts, confirm airflow and refrigerant charge, and catch small problems before July heat turns them into breakdowns. Pair it with a Hammer Mechanical service plan and your whole home's systems get looked after on one schedule.
