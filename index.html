Electrical Services

From panel upgrades and rewiring to pot lights and EV chargers, our licensed electricians handle every job to code. ESA permits and inspections are arranged for you, so every circuit is safe, labelled and done right the first time.

Plumbing Services

Licensed plumbers for repairs, drain cleaning, fixture installs and full renovation rough-ins. We find leaks fast, clear stubborn clogs, and keep the water in your home flowing exactly where it should.

Heating Services

From new heating systems to fast repairs or replacements, we keep every Hamilton home warm through the cold winter. A licensed technician installs and services furnaces, boilers and thermostats, with high efficiency models that lower your utility bill.

Cooling & Air Conditioning

Certified technicians install and repair every kind of AC unit, from a high efficiency central air conditioner to ductless mini splits. If your air conditioning unit blows warm air, we diagnose the real cause and restore dependable cooled air fast.

Heat Pump Systems

One energy efficient heat pump system delivers heating and cooling year round while cutting energy costs in every season. We design, install and optimize cold climate heat pumps built for Southern Ontario winters.

Indoor Air Quality

Better indoor air quality starts with air purification, HEPA filtration, balanced humidity levels and fresh air ventilation. We reduce dust and allergens throughout the house so your family breathes cleaner air.

Ductwork & Ventilation

Leaky air ducts cause energy loss and hot and cold spots around the house. Our duct design, duct sealing and duct cleaning services restore strong airflow so your home heats and cools evenly.

Water Heating

A tankless water heater delivers endless hot water on demand with real energy savings, while a well maintained hot water tank provides dependable stored hot water. We handle water heater installation, repairs and upgrades across Hamilton.

Smart Home & Controls

Smart thermostats, zoning systems and control upgrades lower energy usage and keep comfort consistent in every room. We install and configure everything so your home responds exactly how you want.

Maintenance, Service Plans & Emergency Response

Preventative maintenance, priority service plans and seasonal inspections extend your systems' lifespan and prevent breakdowns. When something fails, our emergency service responds fast across Hamilton and Southern Ontario, day or night.
