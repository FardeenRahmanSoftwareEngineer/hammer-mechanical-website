Water Heater Installation (Tank & Tankless)

Whether a storage tank fits your household or an on-demand tankless unit makes more sense, we size the system to how your family actually uses hot water. TSSA-certified gas technicians handle the fuel and venting work, and every install leaves with proper connections and settings dialed in for steady temperatures from day one.

Water Heater Repair & Maintenance

Lukewarm showers, rumbling tanks, or no hot water at all — we get to the root cause quickly and tell you plainly what failed. Hamilton's hard water is tough on heaters, so regular flushing and maintenance pays off in efficiency and lifespan. When a repair no longer makes financial sense, we lay out replacement options without pressure.

Expansion Tank Installation

In a closed plumbing system, heated water has nowhere to expand — and that pressure hammers your water heater, valves, and fixtures. A correctly sized expansion tank absorbs those spikes, protecting your equipment and extending its working life. We install and pressure-set them properly so the protection is real, not just a tank on a pipe.

Mixing Valve Installation

A thermostatic mixing valve lets you store water hot enough to stay sanitary while delivering it at a safe, scald-free temperature at the tap. It's a small upgrade that matters most in homes with kids or older family members. We install and calibrate mixing valves so every fixture gets consistent, comfortable hot water.
