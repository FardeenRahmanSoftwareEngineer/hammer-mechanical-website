Heating Services in Hamilton

Licensed heating pros for every Hamilton home, from properly sized furnace and boiler installations to fast, honest repairs that keep you warm through a Southern Ontario winter.

Call Now: (905) 555-0123 Request a Quote

Furnace Installation (Gas, Electric, Oil)

We size every furnace to your actual home, not a guess, so you get steady heat and lower gas bills for years. TSSA-registered gas work, carbon monoxide checks and a clean, tidy install come standard, along with a plain-language walkthrough of your new system before we leave.

Furnace Repair & Diagnostics

No heat, weak airflow, strange smells or a furnace that won't stop cycling: our licensed technicians find the actual fault and quote the fix before any work starts. If repair and replacement are both on the table, we lay out the honest numbers and let you decide.

High-Efficiency Furnace Upgrades

Furnaces past the 15-year mark burn more fuel for less comfort, and Hamilton winters make that expensive fast. We walk you through today's high-efficiency options, size the right model for your home, and handle any available rebate paperwork so the upgrade pays for itself sooner.

Boiler Installation & Repair

From classic radiator systems to modern combi boilers that heat your home and your hot water from one unit, we install and service them all. Because Hammer handles plumbing and gas under one roof, your boiler job never needs a second contractor.

Thermostat Installation & Programming

A thermostat that's wired or configured wrong wastes heat all season. We install everything from simple programmable models to full smart thermostats, set the schedules with you, and make sure your furnace, A/C and heat pump all respond the way they should.

Annual Heating Maintenance

A yearly tune-up catches cracked heat exchangers, worn igniters and clogged filters before they become no-heat calls in January. Ask about our maintenance plans, which keep your furnace, boiler or heat pump inspected, cleaned and running safely all year.
