What Counts as a Home Emergency?

Plenty of electrical, plumbing and HVAC problems can wait for a scheduled appointment, but some put your home or your family at risk the longer they run. If you're facing any of the situations below, call us right away and we'll get a licensed tradesperson moving toward your door.

No Heat in Freezing Temperatures

When a furnace, boiler or heat pump quits during a Southern Ontario cold snap, the temperature inside drops fast and frozen pipes can follow within hours. Treat a dead heating system in winter as an emergency: call us and we'll prioritize your home before the cold does real damage.

No Cooling During a Heat Wave

Extreme indoor heat is genuinely dangerous for young children, seniors and pets, not just uncomfortable. If your air conditioner or heat pump gives out in the middle of a Hamilton heat wave, we'll bump your call to the front of the line and work to restore cooling the same day.

Power Loss, Tripping Breakers & Burning Smells

A panel that keeps tripping, outlets that spark, a burning smell near switches, or half the house suddenly without power are all signs of an electrical fault that shouldn't wait. Leave the affected circuits off and call us; our licensed electricians will trace the fault and make it safe, with ESA-compliant repairs.

Burst Pipes & Water Leaks

A burst pipe can dump hundreds of litres into your home in minutes, wrecking floors, drywall and anything below it. Shut off your main water valve if you can reach it safely, then call us; our plumbers respond fast to stop the leak, make the repair, and help you limit the damage.

Frozen Pipes & Frozen Lines

Frozen water lines, iced-over refrigerant lines, or a heat pump locked in a block of ice all need attention before something splits or fails for good. Power the affected system down, leave a faucet cracked open on a frozen water line, and call us before the thaw turns into a flood.

Carbon Monoxide Alarms & Gas Smells

If a carbon monoxide alarm sounds or you smell gas, get everyone out of the house first: call 911 and your gas utility before anything else. Once everyone is safe, call us and our TSSA-registered technicians will find the source and make sure the system is safe before it runs again.

Fast Response Across Hamilton & Southern Ontario

When you call, tell us what you're seeing, hearing or smelling and we'll walk you through immediate safety steps, like shutting off the water main, killing a breaker, or powering down the furnace, before we arrive. Because our team covers electrical, plumbing and HVAC under one roof, one dispatch handles whatever the emergency turns out to be, and our trucks are stocked to finish most repairs in a single visit.
