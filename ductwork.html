Ductwork & Ventilation in Hamilton

Your ducts are the delivery system for every dollar you spend on heating and cooling. Hammer Mechanical designs, installs, seals, and balances ductwork across Hamilton and Southern Ontario so every room gets the airflow it deserves.

Duct Design & Installation

A furnace or heat pump is only as good as the ducts feeding it, so we size and route every run for balanced, quiet airflow before a single piece of sheet metal goes up. Whether it's a new build, a basement finish, or an addition in the Hamilton area, our installers fit ductwork cleanly and support it properly so comfort stays consistent from room to room.

Duct Cleaning & Sealing

Leaky joints and dusty duct runs quietly drain energy and create hot and cold spots long before you notice a problem on your bill. We inspect the whole distribution system, seal leaks where it genuinely improves comfort and efficiency, and clean out the dust and debris that renovations and years of use leave behind.

Dryer Vent Installation & Cleaning

A crushed, over-long, or lint-packed dryer vent makes laundry take forever and is one of the most common household fire hazards. We install short, smooth vent runs the way they should be done and clean existing ones to restore safe airflow. If drying times are creeping up or the laundry room feels muggy, it's time to have the vent path checked.

Air Balancing Services

When the upstairs bakes in July while the main floor stays cool, the fix is usually balance, not bigger equipment. We measure airflow at each register, adjust dampers, and correct restrictions so conditioned air is shared evenly through the whole house. The result is steady comfort in every room instead of one perfect spot and complaints everywhere else.
